Principles of Minimalism

Simplicity

Minimalist design uses fewer elements to create a stronger impact. Every color, shape, image, and piece of text has a clear purpose.

Function over design

In minimalism, design choices are guided by usability. Elements exist because they help communicate a message, improve interaction, or support the user’s experience.

The Beauty of white space

White space, also called negative space, is the empty area around design elements. It allows content to breathe and helps users focus on important information.
